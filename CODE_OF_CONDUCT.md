# Code of Conduct

We want participation in this project to be safe, respectful, and productive.

Be considerate, assume good intent while evaluating technical claims against
evidence, and welcome people regardless of background or experience. Harassment,
threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate disclosure of
private information, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
behavior harms the community. Report conduct concerns privately through the
maintainer contact documented by the hosting repository. Reports will be handled
as confidentially and promptly as practical.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is representing the project.
